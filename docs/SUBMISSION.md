## One-paragraph summary
RenewAI Orchestrator helps an operator of a renewable and battery portfolio decide what to do every 15 minutes. A deterministic Salesforce engine chooses among nine candidate plans under hard limits and a chosen objective, explains why, simulates two hours ahead, and routes risky plans to a human approver. An Agentforce agent and a React app are two ways to use the same engine.
